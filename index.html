Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

1.0

Red Alert

Red Alert will be imposed to deal with any security threat. The situations/circumstances warranting imposition of Red Alert could be:

  1. Any civil disturbance/riots in any part of the city/contiguous areas.
  2. Any act of major terrorism/extremism in any Part of the country.
  3. Sensitive occasions, such as 15 August, 26 January, etc.
  4. Any other situation having security threat on NMRC.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

1.0

During the period of Red Alert

During the period of Red Alert, staff will have to be extremely alert and vigilant. Staff will have to play an active role to protect/guard the passengers and the system. All entry/exit points of the system and vital installations have to be adequately guarded.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

2.0

Red Alert Code will be imposed in the following manner

DGM/Ops, depending upon the situation as mentioned above and on advice of Civil/law enforcing agencies/GM(O)/any other authority, will impose Red alert (with the approval of Executive Director /MD) and inform OCC, Security Control & Security Manager immediately.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

2.0

Red Alert Code (continued)

In case of non-availability of Executive Director/MD, in extreme urgency only, GM/O will impose the red Alert and he will immediately inform MD, Directors & Chief Security Officer about the imposition of Red Alert.

GM/O, in consultation with Chief Security Officer (CSO), will quickly assess the additional requirement of Security staffs & concern Security agencies if any & requisition them in position. The deployment of security officers and other staff at stations & OCC will be done by DGM/Ops in consultation with GM/O on case to case basis.

Red Alert Procedure

3.0

Role of Chief controller/OCC

  1. Chief controller in turn will inform Security Officer and all other controllers, i.e. Security Controller/In-charge, Traction Power Controller, Rolling Stock Controller, Fault Management Controller, CSS, Depot Controllers and CDI/Crew Controller.
  2. He will then inform all Station Managers/Controllers.
  3. Chief Controller should alert Fire Officer and also nearby fire stations.
  4. Chief Controller should also inform ED/GM/DGM and concerned HOD of the project wing.
  5. The morning train services should commence only after Pilot trains cover the entire section.
  6. He will make centralized display of security related messages and announcements at all stations at regular intervals.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

3.1

Role of Station Managers and Staff

  1. Station Managers/Controller will alert all his staff and will verify that the Security personnel (in-house private security agency & concerned Security agency deployed at the station) are vigilant.
  2. Station Manager/Controller will make complete round of his station and inspect important installations, equipments, entry/exit gates, emergency gates, fire exit gates at regular intervals.
  3. Station Manager/Controller will ensure that no public interface point is left unmanned.
  4. Station Manager/Controller will keep vigil on vulnerable points & inform OCC if any assistance is required.
  5. Station Manager/Controller will verify that all public announcement systems & public information display systems are working properly so that they can be used in case the need arises.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

3.1

Role of Station Managers and Staff (continued)

  1. Staff in Customer Care should keep surveillance on passengers entering/exiting from AFC Gates.
  2. Staff on TOM while dealing with passengers will remain extra vigilant.
  3. Station Manager/Controller will verify that all the housekeeping staff are in possession of Identity Cards and have submitted police verification reports with Station Managers/Controllers.
  4. Station Manager/Controller will sensitize parking contractor regarding imposition of Red Alert.

Red Alert Procedure

3.2

Role of In-charge Police Security Controller/In-charge Room at OCC

  1. He will inform OCC about any security threat/alerts.
  2. He will immediately inform to the Chief Security Officer.
  3. He will coordinate with Security Officer and inform Security Agency & Station Security Personnel of all Stations.
  4. He will monitor the security position of all stations through security staff/Security agency deployed at stations.
  5. He will monitor the security position of all the Depot Entry points through security staff/Security agency deployed at Depot Entry Points.
  6. He will assess the security aspect of all the vital installations (viz OCC, sub-stations, feeding posts), SCR, ASS, SER & Telecom room) at the stations/Depot, P. Way installations (Bridges, Viaduct etc) and will communicate, requirement/need of additional security at any such location to the Security Officer.

Red Alert Procedure

3.3

Role of TPC

  1. TPC will inform to HOD and In-charges of project wing, officers of O&M, and key In-charge supervisors immediately about the same.
  2. He will inform all points of vital installation such as ASS/RSS/TSS Plant rooms etc.
  3. E&M staff at stations will have to be in position close to E&M installations.
  4. OHE & P Way supervisors will carry out foot plate inspections more intensively.
  5. The JE/Maintainer at RSS/TSS shall at an interval of every two-hour after a walk around, give feedback to the TPC regarding the status of the installation.

Red Alert Procedure

3.4

Role of FMC

  1. FMC will inform Dy HOD & HOD, other officers and key/In-charge supervisors.
  2. After the staff in Signalling Equipment Rooms and Telecom Equipment Rooms.
  3. Signalling supervisors will carry out footplate inspections more intensively.
  4. Signalling, Telecom & AFC Staff will have to be in position close to their installations at stations.
  5. He will ensure that PA/PIDS & CCTV at all locations are in working order.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

3.5

Role of RSC

  1. RSC will inform GM/RS, all DGM/RS, PPIO of depot, other officers and key/In-charge supervisors.
  2. The trains shall be physically checked by security staff with the assistance of PPIO staff before dispatch on mainline. Any suspicious movement inside depot complex shall be reported immediately to security staff, RSC, DGM/RS. In the absence of security staff, such checks will be conducted by maintenance staff.
  3. Will coordinate deployment of technical staff in the revenue service trains during Red Alert period.
  4. Staff deputed on train will remain in contact with the Train Operator and will also be vigilant during duty.
  5. The breakdown staff should be fully mobilized, kept ready standby.

Red Alert Procedure

3.6

Role of DCC

  1. Chief Depot Controllers/ Depot Controllers should inform all the train operators about the Red Alert while they take possession of trains in depot.
  2. Chief Depot Controller should alert all their staff (i.e. Pointsmen, Shunting, Zamadars, etc.) about the Red Alert. Entire staff should remain vigilant while performing shunting movement in depot area.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

3.7

Role of Train Operators

  1. Train Operators will check the entire train thoroughly while taking possession of train in depot.
  2. Train Operators will be more alert while driving the trains and keep a watch on the track and other installations throughout.
  3. In case any suspicious article on the track or its vicinity is located by them the same will be informed by them to Traffic Controller immediately.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

3.8

Role of Security Staff

  1. Security Officer will alert all concerned security agencies deployed at Metro.
  2. Frisking will be done more rigorously ensuring that no one enters the system including Depot without this and no hand baggage to be allowed without manual or machine screening.
  3. Security Officer will ensure through the Security Controller/In-charge that adequate security staff is deployed at Metro Stations and Depot. If any deficiency/shortage is noticed, should be brought into the notice of Senior NMRC officials.
  4. Security Officer will make random checks at the Stations/Depot/other installations with specific emphasis on the working of security staff.
  5. Security Officer will take feedback at regular intervals from Security Controller/In-charge that Security Staffs deployed at various locations/teams are vigilant and keep Chief Security Officer (CSO) advised. Any laxity to be reported by him to Chief Security Officer (CSO) immediately.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.
Red Alert Procedure

3.9

Role of Project Wing

  1. Concerned HOD of the project wing on receiving information about the imposition of ‘Red Alert’ will in turn direct the staff under them to take necessary security measures at the construction sites by deploying security guards as required.
  2. If any construction work is going on in the operational area of the Stations then it should be ensured by the concerned supervisors that all the working staff at the site is in possession of valid Identity Cards.
  3. Construction supervisors will exercise extra vigilance on the contractors’ staff working at the sites and should ensure that no new staff/worker is employed during the period.

Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.

4–6

Further instructions

  1. After imposition of the Red Alert the concerned controllers shall take the position and readiness of the emergency maintenance vehicles and availability of the operators shall be ensured.
  2. In the underground corridor the concerned Station Controller should take a report for locking of the plant rooms/other technical rooms and report back to Chief Controller.
  3. During the period of ‘Red Alert’, no visitors will be allowed to the OCC, Car Depots and technical areas of the stations without being authorized by ED/MD.

7

Withdrawal of Red Alert

  1. After ascertaining that Red Alert can be withdrawn by Chief Security Officer (CSO)/GM(O)/DGM(O) in consultation with Security agencies.
  2. He will then inform OCC and Security Officer for restoring normalcy.
  3. Chief Controller in turn, will inform all other Controllers, Station Managers/Controllers about the same.
  4. Likewise respective controllers will inform all concerned.
